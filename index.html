Family Rummy

Privacy policy and support · Last updated November 9, 2026

Privacy

Family Rummy does not collect, store, or share any personal information. There are no accounts, ads, analytics, or tracking, and the app never sends anything to a server.

Games are played only between phones in the same place. The app uses your local network, Wi-Fi, and Bluetooth to find nearby phones running Family Rummy and to send game moves directly between them.

The name you type in is saved only on your phone, and it is shown only to the other players at your table. Your phone also saves a random player ID and your current game so you can rejoin or resume. Deleting the app removes all of it.

Because the app collects nothing, it is suitable for children. It has no chat and no way to contact people outside your table.

Support

Can't find a table? Make sure everyone is in the same room with Wi-Fi and Bluetooth turned on. When the app asks to find devices on your local network, tap Allow. If you said no earlier, turn it on in Settings → Privacy & Security → Local Network → Family Rummy.

Someone's phone dropped out? Have them open Family Rummy again and join the same table. They'll get their seat and hand back.
